# Example 3 - Return Policy

**Customer:** I bought an instrument online and changed my mind. Can I return it?

**Agent:** Yes! You can request a return within 7 calendar days after receiving your order, without providing a reason.

The product must be:
- In its original packaging.
- Unused.
- Complete with all accessories and manuals.

The refund will be issued using the original payment method within 10 business days.

Return shipping is covered by the store.
